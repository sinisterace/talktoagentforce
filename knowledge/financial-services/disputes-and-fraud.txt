DISPUTES AND FRAUD

This document explains what happens when you do not recognize a charge, when
a card is lost or stolen, and how a dispute investigation runs. Amounts and
timelines here are written in words because this text is read aloud.

A DISPUTE VERSUS A MERCHANT PROBLEM

A dispute is for a charge you did not authorize, a charge for something you
never received, a duplicate charge, or a charge for the wrong amount. If you
did authorize the purchase and the problem is with the product or the
service, the fastest path is usually to contact the merchant first, because
they can refund directly. Either way the bank can open a dispute if the
merchant does not make it right.

FILING A DISPUTE

When a dispute is filed the bank opens a case and gives you a case number.
The charge is marked as disputed while it is investigated. You do not have
to pay the disputed amount while the case is open.

PROVISIONAL CREDIT

For most card disputes the bank issues a provisional credit within ten
business days while it investigates. That credit is temporary: if the
investigation finds the charge was valid, the credit is reversed and you are
told before that happens. If the dispute is upheld, the credit becomes
permanent.

HOW LONG AN INVESTIGATION TAKES

A card dispute investigation generally completes within forty-five days.
More complex cases, such as a new account or a point-of-sale debit dispute,
can take up to ninety days. You are notified of the outcome in writing.

A LOST OR STOLEN CARD

If a card is lost or stolen, the most important step is to lock it right
away so it cannot be used. Once it is locked, any charges you did not make
can be disputed and a replacement card ordered. Reporting promptly matters:
when a lost or stolen card is reported before any unauthorized charges post,
you owe nothing. The longer an unauthorized charge goes unreported, the more
limited the protection becomes, so reporting the same day is always best.

WHAT HELPS AN INVESTIGATION

For a disputed charge it helps to have the date, the amount, and the
merchant name as it appears on the statement. For something never received,
any order confirmation or tracking is useful. None of this is required to
open the dispute; it only helps resolve it faster.

CARD AFTER A DISPUTE

Filing a dispute does not automatically cancel a card. If the concern is
that the card number itself is compromised, the card should be locked and
replaced in addition to disputing the individual charges.
