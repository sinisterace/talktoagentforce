BRANCHES, HOURS, AND SUPPORT

This document covers branch hours, what genuinely needs an in-person visit,
and how to reach support. Times are written in words because this text is
read aloud.

BRANCH HOURS

Branches are open Monday through Friday from nine in the morning to five in
the afternoon, and Saturday from nine to one. Branches are closed Sunday and
on federal holidays. Drive-up teller windows open a half hour earlier on
weekdays.

PHONE SUPPORT

General support is available seven in the morning to eleven at night Eastern,
seven days a week. The fraud and lost-card line is open twenty-four hours a
day, every day, because a lost card cannot wait for business hours.

WHAT NEEDS A BRANCH

Most things are handled by phone or in the app. A branch visit is genuinely
needed for a few things: a notarized document, a cashier's check above the
app's limit, opening a safe deposit box, and some identity verification
steps for a new account. A wire transfer can be started in a branch or by
phone but not in the app.

APPOINTMENTS

An appointment is not required for teller service but is recommended for
anything that takes time, such as opening an account, a loan consultation,
or a notary. Same-day appointments are usually available.

ACCESSIBILITY

Branches are wheelchair accessible and offer assistive services on request.
The phone line offers support for the hearing impaired through a relay
service, and the mobile app supports screen readers.

NOTARY AND WIRE DESK

Notary service is free for customers and available during branch hours by
appointment. The wire desk closes fifteen minutes before the branch to allow
same-day wires to be processed before the cutoff.
