CARDS AND DIGITAL BANKING

This document covers locking and replacing a card, how quickly a new card
arrives, travel notices, and what the mobile app can do. Amounts and
timelines are written in words because this text is read aloud.

LOCKING A CARD

Locking a card stops new purchases, ATM withdrawals, and cash advances
immediately. Payments you have already scheduled, like a recurring bill on
that card, are not affected while it is locked, and neither are refunds or
deposits. A lock is instant and can be turned off again if the card turns up.
Locking is the right first step any time a card is missing or you see
activity you do not recognize.

REPLACING A CARD

A replacement card arrives by standard mail in five to seven business days at
no charge. Expedited shipping is available for a fifteen dollar fee and
arrives in one to two business days. A new card has a new number, so any
merchant you have set up to bill that card automatically will need the new
number once it arrives.

WHILE YOU WAIT FOR A NEW CARD

The card in the mobile wallet updates to the new number automatically once
the replacement is issued, so tap-to-pay and online purchases through the
wallet keep working before the physical card arrives.

TRAVEL

A travel notice is no longer required. The bank uses the card location and
spending patterns to recognize travel. It is still worth locking the card
promptly if it goes missing on a trip, and a replacement can be shipped to a
temporary address.

CONTACTLESS AND DIGITAL WALLET

Contactless tap-to-pay has a per-transaction limit of two hundred fifty
dollars at most merchants before a PIN or a phone unlock is required. There
is no daily cap on wallet spending beyond the account's own daily purchase
limit.

WHAT THE APP DOES

The mobile app locks and unlocks a card, shows recent transactions, disputes
a charge, orders a replacement, and sets travel and spending alerts. A few
things still need a call or a branch: raising a daily limit, a wire transfer,
and disputing a charge older than sixty days.
