SERVICE AREA, HOURS, SCHEDULING, AND BILLING

BUSINESS HOURS

Regular service hours are 7 AM to 7 PM local time, Monday through Friday. Saturday
hours are 8 AM to 4 PM local time for scheduled maintenance. Emergency dispatch is
available twenty-four hours a day, every day of the year, including holidays.
Office and dispatch staff answer calls during regular hours; outside those hours,
emergency calls reach the on-call dispatcher.

APPOINTMENT WINDOWS

Scheduled appointments are booked as arrival windows, not exact times, because
job durations vary. Standard windows are three hours: 8 AM to 11 AM, 11 AM to
2 PM, 2 PM to 5 PM, and a 5 PM to 7 PM evening window on weekdays. Commercial
maintenance is scheduled in the morning window by default to avoid peak building
occupancy, and can be scheduled outside business hours by arrangement.

We call or text roughly thirty minutes before arrival. Someone eighteen or older
must be present and able to grant access to the equipment for the full window.

RESCHEDULING AND CANCELLATION

An appointment can be rescheduled or cancelled at no charge up to two hours before
the start of the window. Inside two hours, or if no one is present when the
technician arrives, a $59 trip charge applies. Emergency dispatches already en
route cannot be cancelled without the trip charge. Rescheduling requires either
the appointment confirmation number or the phone number the appointment was booked
under.

SERVICE AREA

We serve four metropolitan clusters. Coverage is determined by postal code, and
availability varies by technician skill — some technicians are residential only,
some are commercial certified, and some cover both. If a caller's postal code
falls outside our coverage, we take the details and a dispatcher calls back with
options rather than dispatching a technician who cannot reach them in time.

Time zones matter for scheduling: all appointment windows are quoted in the local
time of the service address, not the time zone of the caller. A customer calling
from one region about a property in another is quoted the property's local time.

FEES

Diagnostic fee: $129 for non-members, $89 for Basic Care members, waived on the
first call of the plan year for Comfort Care, and always waived for Comfort Care
Plus. The diagnostic fee covers the technician's time to identify the problem and
is credited toward the repair if the customer approves the work the same day.

After-hours emergency dispatch surcharge: $145. Waived for Comfort Care Plus and
Commercial Priority members.

Missed-appointment or late-cancellation trip charge: $59.

Repair labor is quoted per job, not hourly, and is discounted by plan tier. Parts
are quoted separately. We provide the full price before starting work, and we do
not begin a repair without approval.

PAYMENT AND WARRANTY

We accept credit card, debit, check, and ACH. Payment is due at completion for
residential work; commercial accounts may be invoiced on net-thirty terms. Labor
on our repairs is warranted for ninety days. Parts we supply and install carry a
one-year warranty, extended to two years for Comfort Care Plus members. New system
installations carry the manufacturer's warranty plus our own labor warranty, which
is documented on the installation agreement.

QUOTES FOR NEW EQUIPMENT

Replacement and new-installation quotes require an on-site load calculation and a
duct assessment; we do not quote system replacement over the phone. A comfort
advisor visit for a replacement quote is free and typically takes ninety minutes.
