HVAC SERVICE PLANS AND COVERAGE

We offer five maintenance plans: three for residential customers and two for
commercial properties. Plans are billed annually and renew on the anniversary of
enrollment. Customers may cancel a plan with thirty days written notice.

RESIDENTIAL PLANS

Basic Care — $189 per year.
Includes one precision tune-up per year, scheduled at the customer's preference
in either spring or fall. Includes a standard one-inch filter replacement at the
time of the visit. Members receive ten percent off all repair labor and priority
scheduling ahead of non-members. Diagnostic fee is $89 for members, $129 for
non-members. Emergency response is available but carries the standard after-hours
surcharge.

Comfort Care — $329 per year.
Includes two precision tune-ups per year, one for the cooling system in spring
and one for the heating system in fall. Includes two filter replacements. Members
receive fifteen percent off all repair labor. The diagnostic fee is waived on the
first service call of each plan year. Emergency response is prioritized above
Basic Care members. Includes a free refrigerant top-off up to one pound per year.

Comfort Care Plus — $549 per year.
Includes everything in Comfort Care, plus: no after-hours surcharge on emergency
calls at any hour, twenty percent off all repair labor, all diagnostic fees
waived, a annual duct inspection, condensate drain treatment, and a two-year
warranty on all parts we install rather than the standard one year. Comfort Care
Plus members are placed at the front of the emergency dispatch queue.

COMMERCIAL PLANS

Commercial Standard — $1,150 per year per rooftop unit.
Includes two scheduled maintenance visits per year, belt and bearing inspection,
coil cleaning, filter replacement on our standard schedule, and a written
condition report after each visit. Fifteen percent off repair labor. Response
target for a no-cool or no-heat call is same business day when reported before
2 PM local time.

Commercial Priority — $1,875 per year per rooftop unit.
Includes four scheduled maintenance visits per year, quarterly filter changes,
economizer and control calibration, refrigerant leak testing, twenty-five percent
off repair labor, and a four-hour emergency response target twenty-four hours a
day including weekends and holidays. Includes an annual efficiency report with
recommendations.

WHAT PLANS DO NOT COVER

Maintenance plans cover inspection, cleaning, adjustment, and the labor discounts
described above. They are not insurance and they are not extended warranties.
Plans do not cover the cost of replacement parts beyond the discount, full system
replacement, ductwork replacement, refrigerant beyond the amounts listed,
thermostat replacement, damage caused by flooding, fire, or power surge, or work
on equipment that was not professionally installed.

UPGRADING AND DOWNGRADING

A customer may upgrade at any time and pay only the prorated difference for the
remainder of the plan year. Upgraded benefits take effect immediately. Downgrades
take effect at the next renewal date, not mid-year.

RENEWAL

We contact plan members approximately thirty days before the renewal date.
Renewing before the anniversary date locks the current year's price for the
following year. Plans that lapse for more than sixty days require a new system
inspection before re-enrollment, which is charged at the standard diagnostic rate.
